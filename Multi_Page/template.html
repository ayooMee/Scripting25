Yeah, you can hit the CN Tower and take the glass floor photo, or catch a Raptors game if you have the cheese for the nosebleeds. But the real attractions, kind of like spider-man, are street level. Kensington Market on a Saturday is a state of controlled chaos in the best possible way, with vintage shops, international food stalls, and street music by REAL musicians creating an atmosphere you won't forget. Queen West got you with art galleries, got you with real boutiques, and cafes where the creative communities like to kick it. Chinatown serves incredible late-night food that hits different after a long day exploring. The food scene alone makes Toronto worth visiting. You can genuinely eat your way around the world without leaving the city, from authentic Somali restaurants in Etobicoke to traditional dim sum in Markham to Caribbean jerk chicken EVERYWHERE in Scarborough that will change your standards forever. Festivals run all summer long! Caribana brings Caribbean culture to the streets with one n some corner google maps somehow hasn't found yet.
